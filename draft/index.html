The consensus

Each board counts at its weight, halved for every 90 days since it came out, so summer boards fade as the season's come in. The bar under each outlet up top shows how much it counts today.

A player a board leaves out counts as one past the end of that board, but no worse than half again his average elsewhere. A Top 30 can't sink someone everyone has at 45.

Range and movement

Range is a player's best and worst rank across the boards, with his consensus spot marked. A wide bar is an argument. Gold numbers flag the biggest ones.

Move is spots gained or lost since the previous update. Open a player for his line over the season.
