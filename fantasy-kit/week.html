Pick the games

The whole week, Sunday's day games, the night games, one game or your own picks. The lineup fits the slate, so three games never ask for three receivers.

Everyone vs. everyone

With a group, the top score wins and everyone is ranked. Or pair off head to head, add a heist, or go best ball.

Run it back

When the week's over, one tap starts next week with the same group. The series keeps score: wins, top-three finishes and points.

Salary cap

No draft at all. Everyone builds a lineup under a $100 cap before the first kickoff, and a player nobody else picked scores 1.25x.

Sealed draft

Rank players in private whenever you like. When the lists lock, the draft runs itself in seconds.

Trivia draft
