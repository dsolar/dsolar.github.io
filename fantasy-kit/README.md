# Fantasy Kit

**One product, one nav.** Draft Lab is the whole fantasy product: its home page and sport pages live in `fantasy/` (hub.js), and the draft room, leagues and quick games in `fantasy-kit/`. Every page shows the same masthead and sections from `nav.js` (`FK.nav.TABS`: Home, Your leagues, Mock draft, Rankings, Quick games, Demo league; `FK.nav.href`), and the brand goes to the home page (`FK.nav.HOME`, `fantasy/`). The home and sport pages add the sport row below it. Add a section in `nav.js` and in the first-paint headers of `index.html`, `leagues.html` and `week.html`. The sections sit on the masthead's own line (`U.shell({ inline: true })`, a second line on phones), the home and sport pages pin their sport row inside the sticky header, and on phones scrolling down tucks the site bar away (`body.fk-tuck`, nav.js). The masthead's **player search** (`FK.nav.findHtml`) reads `data/search.json` (every player in the six pools, `tools/fantasykit_search.py`, last step of the data run) and opens a player's card through the rankings page (`?s=<sport>&p=<id>#rankings`). Signed in, the home page lists **your leagues** with this week's matchup for up to four head-to-head leagues, scored by the league engine in the browser (`fantasy-kit/home.js`, loaded only then). Sports are labeled NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, NCAAB, NCAAF (`sport.short`). **Logos** come from ESPN's image server: league logos for the pro sports (`S.leagueLogo`; there's none for the NCAA, so college keeps its drawn icon), conference logos on the conference picker (`S.confLogo`), and team or school logos where a player has no photo. **Small samples** (`U.sampleOf`: under 40% of the most games anyone in the pool has played, value.js `gamesMax`) get a games tag by the name and a line on the player card.

The shared parts of every fantasy league page on davidsolar.dev: player data for basketball, football, baseball and hockey, player values, a draft engine, a season engine, and the screens that sit on top of them. The look blends Yahoo and ESPN density for tables with Sleeper polish for matchups, trades and the draft room, in light and dark.

- **Draft Lab** (`index.html`, `lab.js`): mock drafts against bots.
- **Demo leagues** (`league.html`, `demo.js`): full leagues with bots and seasons that move in real time. Each sport has a head to head league and a best ball guillotine league (`?f=g`).
- **Hard Cap** (`../hardcap/`) uses the kit's styles, shell, matchup hero, feeds, trade window and draft room layout with its own rules.

Plain browser scripts, no build step. `sports.js`, `value.js` and `draft.js` have no DOM and no network, so Node tests and a Supabase edge function can load them too.

## Player data

Every sport uses one shape:

```json
{ "sport": "mlb", "season": "2026", "source": "...",
  "lines": { "bat": { "label": "Batting", "cols": ["g", "pa", "ab", "r", ...] }, "pit": { ... } },
  "players": [{ "id": "660271", "n": "Shohei Ohtani", "t": "LAD", "pos": ["UT", "SP"], "l": { "bat": [...], "pit": [...] } }] }
```

- **Basketball:** per-game lines, this season blended with last season (last season counts like 20 games), from Hard Cap's ESPN pull. Positions and ages come from `celtics/data.json`.
- **Baseball:** season totals from MLB's Stats API files in `tools/mlb/`. Values pace a player who missed time toward a full season, so 285 plate appearances counts like about 470.
- **Football:** season totals and Sleeper's fantasy points, plus Sleeper's season projection when there is one. Until the workflow first reaches Sleeper, the file is seeded from the guillotine league, without kickers or defenses.
- **Hard Cap (real contracts):** a toggle on any NBA or NHL season league (and the mock draft). `data/contracts-nba.json` and `contracts-nhl.json` (`tools/fantasykit_contracts.mjs`, in the data run) hold each player's salary by season: the NBA from the shared contracts library (`nba/lib/contracts.js`, over `celtics/data.json` and `hardcap/stats.json`), standard deals only; the NHL from the NHL hub's cap hits. The league's line (`hardCap.line`) is a multiple of the real cap or the suggested line: what the players the league will roster are paid, shared out evenly. The draft (`draft.js capRoom`) keeps room to fill the roster at the minimum and bots weigh price against room; adds and trades (`league.js canRoster`, `tradeCheck`) stay under the line (a trade may not leave a team further over). A dynasty carries contracts: at the new season an expiring deal is re-signed (held at the minimum until the player's new real deal is in the data) or let go into the rookie and free agent draft (`season.js nextHardCap`, `L.next.letGo`). The old standalone Hard Cap app at `/hardcap/` redirects here.
- **IDP (individual defensive players):** an NFL lineup with DL, LB, DB or IDP spots (the "IDP" preset) plays defenders too. They live in their own files so other leagues load nothing extra: `data/nfl-idp.json` (Sleeper's defenders, folded into DL/LB/DB, in `nfl.json`'s columns, with their IDP points in the `pts_*` columns: 1 a solo tackle, 0.5 an assist, 2 a sack, 1 a TFL, 0.5 a QB hit, 3 an interception, 2 a forced fumble or recovery, 1 a pass defended, 6 a TD, 2 a safety) and `data/weeks-nfl-idp.json` (each week's lines in a short column list). Pages, setup and the league clock merge them in with `S.addIdp` and `S.addIdpWeeks`. Sleeper's live feed has no defenders, so their week lines update with the data runs (every two hours on game days), not live. Custom points can weight each IDP stat.
- **College basketball:** `data/cbb.json` from the CBB Hub (`tools/cbb_dfs.py`, run by the college basketball workflow): every player with real minutes, guards and forwards as DraftKings lists them, a season per-game line (`pg`, its `eg` set to 0, so small samples count `gp`) and a next-game projection (`ppg`) that scales his line by his team's projected points. Its `teams` carry each school's conference. **One-day games:** the night's slates are the hub's 14 most watchable games (`cbb/data/dfs.json`), served by fk-live `?sport=cbb&slates=1` (`supabase/functions/fk-live/cbb.mjs`); box scores come from fk-live `?sport=cbb&games=`, keyed by ESPN athlete id. **Season leagues, mock drafts and rankings:** scorings are DraftKings points, standard points and 9 categories (custom categories and presets are the NBA's without plus/minus, which college box scores lack). `weeks-cbb.json` is built by `tools/fantasykit_cbb.mjs` in the Fantasy Kit data run: every Division I game on ESPN's scoreboard (`groups=50`), box scores through fk-live's `cbbBox`, summed Monday to Sunday for pool players, weeks numbered from the Monday on or before Nov 2; a week is final the morning after its last day once every game is over, and open weeks' games are cached in `tools/cbb_fantasy/games.json`. fk-live `?sport=cbb` is the live weekly feed (at most 90 new box scores a request, live games first; `partial` until a cold week is filled in). Setup's **Player pool** picks conferences (one, several, the power five, or every school): the league stores their schools as `season.pool` / `settings.pool` (read by `S.poolOf`) and the names as `confs`. **Season ends** stops the league before the conference tournaments, on Selection Sunday (the default) or after the title game (`S.collegeEnd`, `settings.collegeEnd`); weeks left are counted to that date. In March a player scores only while his team is still playing. `tools/fantasykit_cbb.sql` adds the sport to the backend.
- **College football:** `data/cfb.json` and `weeks-cfb.json` come from `tools/fantasykit_cfb.mjs` in the Fantasy Kit data run (and on Saturday afternoons and nights): ESPN's box score of every FBS game, read by `supabase/functions/fk-live/cfb.mjs` (`cfbBox`) in the NFL's column names (`S.CFB_COLS`, plus `ret_td`), so the NFL's scorings, categories (those the box score can make: no targets, first downs, two-point tries or EPA) and custom points apply. Points are worked out per game (0.04 a passing yard, 4 a passing TD, 0, 0.5 or 1 a catch, 3 a field goal and 5 from 50+, defenses by points allowed tier); `fgm_50p` counts at most one long field goal a game, and a defense's TDs are the larger of its defensive and interception-return counts. Weeks are ESPN's regular-season weeks (Monday to Sunday; week 1 is the long opening stretch), then the postseason in Monday-to-Sunday weeks from 16; final weeks are kept, open weeks' games cached in `tools/cfb_fantasy/`. Teams and conferences come from the College Football Hub (`cfb/data/hub.json`). The pool is FBS quarterbacks, backs, receivers, tight ends, kickers and team defenses (id = the school's abbreviation). `S.football(k)` is football's way of playing (one game a week, lineups locking by game, per-game projections) for both `nfl` and `cfb`. Leagues pick conferences like college hoops (the power four quick pick) and a **Season ends**: after rivalry week, after the conference championships (default) or after the national title game (`S.collegeEnd(kind, t, "cfb")`). fk-live `?sport=cfb` is the live weekly feed. `tools/fantasykit_cfb.sql` adds the sport to the backend (applied).
