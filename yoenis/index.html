                            League rules

                            • 16 teams. The $125 buy-in is due by Venmo before the draft, which makes a $2,000 pot.
                            • Head-to-head categories. Hitting: runs, hits, home runs, RBI, steals, total bases, average and OPS. Pitching: wins, losses, saves, strikeouts, holds, ERA, WHIP and quality starts.
                            • Eight teams make the playoffs, which run the last three weeks of the season with reseeding.
                            • Up to 7 pickups a week, and at least 30 innings pitched a week. Waivers use a free agent budget.
                            • Playoff champion $600, runner-up $400, third place $200.
                            • Best regular-season record $500, second best $300.
                            • Keep up to 5 players, each for up to 3 seasons in a row, no matter who keeps him. After the third, he goes back into the draft.
                            • Year one costs the round he was drafted in. Year two costs two rounds earlier, and year three costs six rounds earlier than year one.
                            • A drafted player always keeps his draft round, whoever drafted him and however he changed teams. Anyone undrafted costs a 15th-round pick.
                            • To be kept, a player has to be on your roster before September 1.
                            • Nothing costs more than round 1, but each first-round keeper needs its own 1st-round pick.
                            • If you have no pick in a keeper's round, or two keepers there and one pick, he moves up to your next open pick. He still counts at his real round the next year.
                            • Keepers lock a week or so before the draft. Declare them in the 2027 keepers tab on this page with your team PIN; they also show up in the keeper sheet.
                            • Both managers confirm a trade on the league email thread.
                            • Every accepted trade goes through a 48-hour counter window. There are no vetoes, but any team can counter. A counter has to be for the same players, with nothing added.
                            • No more than two players from either team, so 2-for-2 at most.
                            • The deadline is in early August. A trade has to be accepted in Yahoo by 11:59 p.m. Pacific that day, and it can finish its counter window after.
                            • Draft picks can be traded only in the offseason. Each side sends the same number of picks, so every team holds 25 at all times.
                            • No swapping picks just to move up or down in a round where you're keeping someone.
                            • 25 rounds, snake order, as close to Opening Day as possible. The league votes on the date.
                            • A lottery sets the order for the eight teams that missed the playoffs. Playoff teams pick 9th through 16th in reverse order of finish, so the champion picks last.

                            New for 2027: declare your keepers right here. Under Keepers and drafts, pick your team, check up to five keepers and save them with a team PIN. Your first save sets the PIN, and the commissioner can reset a forgotten one. Vote on the draft date under 2027 offseason, Draft day, by checking every date you can make. Both feed the keeper sheet.
