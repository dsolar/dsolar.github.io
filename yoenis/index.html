            League rules

            Basics

            • 16 teams, $125 buy-in.
            • Head-to-head categories. Hitting: runs, hits, home runs, RBI, steals, total bases, average and OPS. Pitching: wins, losses, saves, strikeouts, holds, ERA, WHIP and quality starts.
            • Eight teams make the playoffs, which run the last three weeks of the season with reseeding.
            • Up to 7 pickups a week, and at least 30 innings pitched a week. Waivers use a free agent budget.

            Keepers

            • Keep up to 5 players, each for up to 3 seasons. After the third, he goes back into the draft.
            • Year one costs the round he was drafted in. Year two costs two rounds earlier, and year three costs six rounds earlier than year one.
            • Free agents count as round 15.
            • Nothing costs more than round 1, but each first-round keeper needs its own 1st-round pick.
            • Two keepers in the same round: one moves up a round unless you own two picks there. If that round is also taken, he keeps moving up to the next open pick.

            Trades

            • Every accepted trade goes through the counter window. There are no vetoes, but any team can counter an accepted trade.
            • No more than two players from either team, so 2-for-2 at most.
            • A trade accepted before the deadline can finish its counter and review period after it.
            • Draft picks can be traded in the offseason, but every team must hold 25 picks at all times.

            Draft

            • A lottery sets the order of the picks for teams that missed the playoffs.
