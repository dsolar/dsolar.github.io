Draft formats

Nine ways to fill a roster. Every one runs in Draft Lab, for all three sports.

Start a mock draft →
SnakeThe order flips every round. The classic.
LinearThe same order every round, so the first pick picks first every time.
Third-round reversalA snake with round three flipped again, to even out the top of the draft.
AuctionA budget and live bidding on every player. Nominate, bid, pass. Bots bid by proxy.
Mystery packagesEvery team gets a random set of picks, like 1, 24 and 42 against 12, 13 and 18, dealt so each set is worth the same.
Pack draftEveryone rips open a pack with a star in it, takes one player and passes the rest, all at once.
White elephantA snake where your turn can steal a drafted player. Stolen players score less, and the team you rob gets a golden make-up pick.
Sealed listsRank players in private. When everyone locks, the draft runs itself from the lists.
Salary capNo draft at all: build a lineup under a budget, DraftKings style. Anyone can pick anyone.

Season formats

How the league is scored, from a full season down to a single game.

Head to headPlay one team a week. Standings, playoffs, FAAB waivers and trades.
H2H categoriesEvery category is its own win or loss each week.
RotisserieSeason totals, ranked in each category. No weekly matchups.
All-playYour score against every team, every week, so the schedule can't rob you.
GuillotineThe lowest score is chopped each week and its roster goes up for bids. Last team standing wins.
Best ballNo lineups to set. Your best possible lineup scores each week. Works with guillotine too.
Quick gamesDraft tonight, play this week's real games, crown a winner. Games before the draft don't count.
One dayPick a slate of tonight's games, draft, and it's scored on those box scores alone.
ShowdownOne game, a captain at 1.5x and five flex. Snake or a $50 auction.
HeistRight after the draft, everyone secretly shields their own starters and steals or jinxes the other team's.

Features and twists

Optional rules on top of any format. Mix and match in Draft Lab or a league with friends.

In the draft

  • Draft-day tradesTrade picks and drafted players while the draft runs. Bots answer, and sometimes call you.
  • Time bankOne clock for the whole draft instead of a pick clock. The time you save becomes FAAB.
  • Team rouletteA wheel picks the pro team you have to draft from, each pick or each round. Re-spins if you want them.
  • Trivia draftAnswer a quick sports question when you're on the clock. Miss it and your pick drops to the end of the round.
  • KeepersKeep players from last year at the cost of a round or a price.
  • Prospect roundsExtra rounds after the main draft for prospects only, who start on the taxi squad.

League structure

  • DynastyRosters carry over, with a rookie draft, a taxi squad and future picks to trade.
  • The splitPartway through the season the table splits in half, and only the top half plays for the title.
  • Promotion and relegationTwo tiers. The bottom of the top league swaps with the top of the lower one each year.
  • Median gameAlso play the league median each week, for a second win or loss.
  • Pick your opponentWorst record first, each team picks who it plays next week.
  • Half the player poolAL-only or NL-only, AFC or NFC, East or West. Only that half can be drafted.

Twists

  • Robin HoodThe week's lowest scorer takes a player from the top scorer and gives one back.
  • ConquestWin your matchup and take a player from the team you beat, giving one back.
  • VampireOne team skips the draft, builds from free agents, and feeds on every team it beats.
  • KingslayerOne team wears the crown. Beat the king to take it and a FAAB bounty that grows every week.
  • The HangoverLast week's winners can't add free agents or put in claims this week.
  • Top-score bonusThe week's top scorer gets extra FAAB, a reason to spend early instead of hoarding.
