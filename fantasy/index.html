Draft formats

All of them run in Draft Lab, for any of the three sports.

  • SnakeThe order flips every round. Linear and third-round reversal are there too.
  • AuctionNominate, bid, and fill a roster on a budget. Bots bid by proxy.
  • Mystery packagesEvery team gets a random set of picks, like 1, 24 and 42 against 12, 13 and 18, dealt so each set is worth the same.
  • White elephantYour turn can be a steal. The team you rob picks again right away.
  • Time bankOne clock for the whole draft. The time you save becomes FAAB.
  • Draft-day tradesTrade picks and players you've drafted while the draft runs. Bots answer, and sometimes call you.

Season formats

The demo leagues play these with bots, a week at a time in real time.

  • Head to headWeekly matchups, standings, playoffs, FAAB waivers and trades with a league vote.
  • GuillotineEveryone scores every week and the lowest team is chopped. Its roster goes up for bids midweek, and the last team standing wins.
  • Best ballNo lineups to set. Each week your best possible lineup scores.
  • Salary capHard Cap's model: real contracts, one hard line and a Prospect Board.

Everything here runs on the Fantasy Kit, one set of player data, draft and season code shared by every league page. How it's built.
