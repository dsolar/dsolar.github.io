CHAMPION GIRL DADS READ THE CONTROLS

Actually walk him home.

Move: arrows or WASD. On a phone, hold the direction buttons below the street. Walk right toward home. Move up and down to dodge pink distractions.

Supplies: green poutine restores energy; green chargers restore phone battery. Walk over them. Pink acquaintances cost time and inflate his ego. At 100 ego, a monologue costs 15 seconds.

Encounters: approach a glowing marker and press E or INTERACT. Pick a reply or press 1–3. Your answer can earn an offer, a rejection, or a bill. Drinks, bathroom invitations, loans, and adult dating invitations can be accepted or declined. Most conversations let you continue. Accepting a stay-over or conversion branch ends the run, with a clearly labeled alternate ending.

Win: reach the mattress before the four-minute grace period expires, with energy left. The timer runs while you walk and pauses during conversations. Choices deduct their stated time costs. Buzz slows walking and increases energy drain. Loans add matching cash and debt; outstanding debt reduces your final score. DA PHONE lets you buy food, buy a short ride, or use a favor. Rides stop at the next conversation. High buzz can end a romantic invitation; unpaid loans block further borrowing. Pause is always available. Leaving the tab pauses play.

Current progress saves on this device. All locations, conversations, and incidents here are fictional game scenes. Dating characters are fictional adults.
