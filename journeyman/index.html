League

How it works

  • You get every team he played for, in order, with the years. A team he went back to shows up again. Each player is worth 5 points.
  • Stuck? Clues cost a point each: position, college or birthplace, draft or debut, and his career numbers. Initials cost 2. A wrong guess costs 1.
  • Typos are free, and so is a last name a few players share. Only a real player who isn't the answer costs you.
  • Everyone here played for at least four franchises and finished his career in 2000 or later.
