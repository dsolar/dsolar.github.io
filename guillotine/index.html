      How the odds work

      This week, each team's expected score is its Yahoo projection. A team without a projection uses its average instead. A team with no starters set is assumed to set them before kickoff. If it doesn't, it scores zero and is cut.

      Later weeks use each team's average over its last three weeks, pulled a quarter of the way toward the league average. Rosters keep changing as cut teams' players go to waivers, so nobody's current form lasts all season.

      Actual scores swing about 22% either way from those numbers. Each simulation plays out the rest of the season, cutting the lowest score every week until one team is left. The page runs 20,000 of them and counts.

      Not in the model yet: waiver pickups and money left. A team with a big budget will improve, and these odds don't know that.
