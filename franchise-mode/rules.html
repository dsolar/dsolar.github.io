Rules & assumptions

A playable foundation

This is an early league-management simulation, not a complete CBA simulator. It uses the same projected player pool as The 31st Franchise. Player contracts, ages and ratings come from that game’s snapshot, not a live NBA roster feed.

What works

  • Expansion roster import, contract rollover, retained future assets, browser saves and JSON backups.
  • Whole-league payroll, projected cap/tax/apron levels and estimated progressive tax, including repeater history accumulated in this save.
  • Waiving players with remaining guaranteed salary retained as dead money with the original year-by-year salary schedule retained.
  • Player-package trades with bilateral salary checks, first-apron incoming salary restrictions, second-apron aggregation checks, and hard caps triggered by modeled trade mechanisms.
  • AI valuation considers player quality, age, positional need, contract value, payroll savings and ownership targets. AI teams seek salary relief and make free-agent offers as you advance market days.
  • Free-agent offers, reserved room, modeled cap holds, renouncing rights, incumbent matching of RFA offer sheets, and a two-day decision window.
  • Regular seasons with 82 games per team, balanced wins and losses, age progression and expiring contracts.

Contract tools

Expand a roster contract to see each year, or open Contracts for a five-season committed-payroll view. Free-agent previews show annual salary, total guarantee, signing method and resulting payroll. Flat new contracts (with future minimum-salary floors) are intentional; the engine does not negotiate annual raises or options. Waiver confirmations show the complete remaining guarantee.

Modeled non-taxpayer, taxpayer and room mid-level pools track spending, including pending offer sheets. Non-taxpayer mid-level use triggers a first-apron hard cap; taxpayer use triggers a second-apron hard cap. Bird re-signings allow five years, ordinary contracts four, taxpayer/minimum exceptions two, and room mid-level three. The engine chooses a legal method automatically. Full Bird tenure is preserved across re-signings and trades. Unknown starting tenure and cap holds remain assumptions.

Simulation assumptions

  • Future cap starts at the expansion game’s $176M for 2027–28, then grows 5% per year. Tax and apron ratios use the Banner 19’s 2026–27 snapshot. These are projections.
  • Expansion teams receive the normal league cap after the imported debut season in this prototype; final future expansion terms are not established here.
  • Budgets are fictional ownership mandates based on roster strength and age. Most teams avoid the second apron; rebuilding teams target the tax line; a few elite teams will spend above the apron. You can change your own mandate.
  • The old game never assigned destinations to NBA free agents removed from its market. Those players remain unassigned on import. The expansion teams’ actual signed players are preserved.
  • Existing non-expansion contract tenure is initialized to two years. RFA labels come from the source pool, with unsigned incumbent RFAs treated as retained for one season before the imported offseason. Rights, cap holds, service years are estimated from age where unavailable, so veteran minimums and service-based maximum salaries are modeled; unknown contract years stay flat. Two-way players enter the available pool.
  • Regular-season outcomes use Banner 19’s shared offense/defense impact, positional rotation and replacement-minute logic. Multi-game absences follow each player’s expected availability, starting from a stationary injury distribution. Injuries can be disabled. Healthy replacement minutes are used directly, without applying another health discount to player talent. AI rosters below 14 receive replacement players before the season. No playoffs or championship is invented.

Not implemented yet

Rookie drafts and pick conveyance/trading, Stepien checks, frozen-pick penalties, exact qualifying offers and player service history, rookie-scale options, player/team options, early-Bird limits, biannual exceptions, trade exceptions carried forward, sign-and-trades, stretch/set-off, salary bonuses, trade kickers, no-trade rights, full trade timing rules, apron salary adjustments, historical repeater tax records, playoffs and complete future roster forecasting. Imported draft assets are retained but cannot be spent. Newly signed players are conservatively trade-locked for the offseason.
