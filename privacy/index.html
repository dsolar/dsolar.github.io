Privacy

Last updated November 5, 2026

davidsolar.dev is a personal site of games and fantasy sports tools. Most of it works without an account and keeps your progress only in your own browser. This page explains what's collected when you sign in or post a score, and what happens to it.

If you don't sign in

Your game progress, streaks and settings stay in your browser's storage on your device. Nothing about them is sent anywhere, except a score you choose to post to a public leaderboard, which shows the display name you type in.

If you make an account or sign in

  • Account details: your email address, a username if you pick one, and your password (stored only as a secure hash). If you sign in with Google, the site receives your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and nothing else from your Google account.
  • Fantasy leagues: the leagues you create or join, your team and moves, chat messages you post, and any profile name or photo you add. Other managers in your league can see these.
  • Daily games: while you're signed in, your daily game results, streaks and leaderboard name are saved to your account so they show up on your other devices.
  • Email: your address is used for sign-in links, password resets and the league alerts you turn on. It's never shown to other managers.

How it's stored

Accounts and saved data are stored with Supabase. Emails are sent through Resend. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and counts page visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites.

What isn't done with it

Your information isn't sold, rented or shared for advertising, and it isn't used for anything beyond running the site's features described above.

Deleting your data

Email hello [at] davidsolar.dev from the address on your account and your account and everything saved with it will be deleted. Signing out removes the saved sign-in from that browser right away.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
