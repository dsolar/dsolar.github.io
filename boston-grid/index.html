        How it works
        • Each square wants a player who played at least one regular-season game for both of its teams. Teams that moved count once: the Sonics are the Thunder, the Oilers are the Titans, the Expos are the Nationals.
        • Nine guesses in all. A wrong guess uses one up; a name the game doesn't know doesn't.
        • A right answer earns 10 to 100 deep-cut points: more for a lesser-known player. Squares decide the board, deep-cut points break the ties.
        • Players since 1990. The MLB data runs through the last complete season. A new grid in each league at midnight, Boston time.
