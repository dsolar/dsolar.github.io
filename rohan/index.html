05 / THE CONSTITUTION

The rules of Rohan.

A quick reference to the current league constitution, including its 2025 amendments.

Read the full constitution ↗
The CHASE for the Championship

The regular season lasts 13 weeks. Six teams qualify, and the top two seeds receive a first-round bye.

Weeks 14–15: Seeds 3–6 compete on cumulative points, with no head-to-head matchups. The two highest-scoring teams advance.

Weeks 16–17: Those two teams join the top two seeds. Highest cumulative score wins the championship; the rest finish in scoring order.

Playoff ties use optimal lineups, then the higher seed. Qualification and seeding ties use total points, then head-to-head record, then record against common opponents.

Keepers & the One-Third Compromise

Keep up to three players. Initial keeper cost comes from the draft or free-agent acquisition price; trades retain the existing keeper price and eligibility.

For the second consecutive keeper season and each year after, move one-third of the way from the previous keeper price toward the current ESPN PPR Value Sheet price, then use standard rounding. Prices do not fall when the projection drops below the keeper cost.

Previous price + (ESPN value − previous price) ÷ 3

Constitution example: A $7 keeper with a $30 projection becomes $15. The following year’s calculation starts from that most recent keeper price.

Players added after the trade deadline cannot be kept. Players added while on IR cannot be kept; the constitution also sets retention rules for full-season IR and suspended players.

Rosters, waivers & auction budgets

Sixteen roster spots: ten starters and six bench spots, plus two IR slots. Each team has $205 at the draft, with keeper costs deducted first.

The free-agent budget is $100. Transactions process at noon Wednesday through Sunday. The former free post-waiver pickup rule was eliminated in 2023.

Trades & league votes

Confirmed trades have a 24-hour review period for counteroffers involving only players from the original deal. If no counteroffer is accepted, the original trade processes.

Keeper trades are allowed in-season and in the offseason. Trading money or draft/FAAB budget is prohibited. The constitution places the trade deadline between NFL Weeks 9 and 10.

Offseason rule changes are put to a league vote; six or more votes pass a proposal. Consult the full document for the collusion provision and amendment history.

Dues & payouts

The annual buy-in is $100. The constitution lists $700 for the champion, $225 for the runner-up, $100 for third, and $75 for the regular-season points leader.

The lowest-scoring regular-season team contributes an additional buy-in to the champion’s purse. An unchecked lineup can incur a $10 weekly penalty, subject to the commissioner’s stated exception for a legitimate excuse.
