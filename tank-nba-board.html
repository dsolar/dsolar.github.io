Determining the NBA Draft Order
1
16-team lottery comprised of: 10 teams that miss the play-in, four 9/10 play-in seeds, and two losers of 7/8 play-in games
2
Lottery balls based on status: non-play-in teams receive 3 balls, 9/10 play-in seeds receive 2 balls, and 7/8 play-in losers receive 1 ball
3
The three teams with the worst overall records are "draft relegated" and penalized by losing one lottery ball (reducing their total from 3 to 2)
4
Picks 1 through 16 are ALL awarded via drawing of lottery balls
5
Draft-relegated teams are guaranteed a top-12 pick
6
Pick restrictions prevent a team's original pick from being 1st overall in consecutive drafts, or a top-five pick in three consecutive drafts
7
7/8 play-in winner and non-play-in playoff teams, sorted by record, are assigned picks 17-30 (playoff seeds and outcomes have no impact)
8
Random drawing decides who picks first between playoff teams with the same record
9
The first 16 picks of the second round are in inverse order of the Lottery drawing
10
The rest of the second round follows the order from picks 17-30 with ties broken in reverse order of the teams' first round picks
