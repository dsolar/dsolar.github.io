How the finder judges an offer

Both teams must gain at least the selected number of projected starting points per week. The balance setting limits how much larger one team’s gain can be. Bench points and original auction prices do not count as starter points.

The search checks eligible one-for-one trades and a shortlist of two-for-one and two-for-two packages. It verifies promising offers with the full analyzer, including required drops, the shared replacement pool, byes, and your keeper rules. It does not check every possible package or predict whether a manager will accept.

Players in IR or reserve slots, players without usable projections, and players whose games have already started are excluded. Choose From next week to include players locked this week. Future weekly values distribute ESPN’s remaining-season forecasts across scheduled games. Keeper planning dollars are estimates and stay separate from the point gains.

ESPN projections · Your league’s scoring

Make the house rules explicit.

Keeper count and auction budget start with ESPN’s settings. Set the cost rules your league uses. These settings affect the keeper comparison and are saved in this browser.

Keeper slots have an opportunity cost.

The comparison selects each roster’s best eligible keeper portfolio within its keeper limit and remaining draft budget. It compares that portfolio before and after the trade.

Original auction prices are imported historical costs. Planning market values are estimates from current projections and league budgets, and can be edited under Auction & player values. They are not forecasts of next year’s auction. An unconfirmed cost rule leaves the keeper verdict incomplete.

Prices, costs, and your assumptions.

Original auction prices come from ESPN’s draft results. Edit next-year costs, planning values, or availability when your house rules or outlook differ.

Full original draft results

Planning values estimate an auction market from current projection value above available replacements. Enter your own expected next-draft values for a keeper view, especially for injured players and long-term bets. Overrides stay attached to player IDs through a proposed trade.
